Floating Lens

Translate anything on your screen

A floating bubble that lives over every app. Tap any text — a message, a caption, a notification, a button — and TypeAura instantly reads and translates it. No copy-paste, no switching apps.

How it works

Tap. Read. Translate.

Turn on the Floating Lens and a small bubble floats above whatever you're using. Tap the text you want to understand and the Lens reads it on the spot, then shows the translation in a clean pop-up — right where you're looking.

It works in WhatsApp, Instagram, browsers, games — literally any app. And it's privacy-safe: the Lens automatically skips password and editable fields, and never stores what it reads.

How to Install

Up and running in 3 steps

1

Get it on Google Play

Tap the button below to open the TypeAura listing on the Play Store, then tap Install.

Get it on Google Play
2

Enable TypeAura

Go to Settings → System → Languages & Input → On-screen keyboard and enable TypeAura.

3

Set as Default

Select TypeAura as your default keyboard when you tap any text field, and enjoy!

Builder Story

Why I built TypeAura

I'm Himanshu Kashyap, a developer who's been frustrated with keyboards that don't understand how Indians actually type. We all mix Hindi and English constantly, but every keyboard out there treats it like a problem instead of a superpower.

So I built TypeAura — a keyboard that doesn't judge your Hinglish, but makes it shine. With AI translation built right into the keyboard, you can type the way you think and sound the way you want.

TypeAura is in beta and improving every week. Your usage and feedback directly shapes what gets built next.
