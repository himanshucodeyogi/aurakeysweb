What is TypeAura?

The keyboard that understands how Indians type

Most Indians type in Hinglish, Hindi, or their regional language — but standard keyboards don't understand it. TypeAura bridges that gap with AI translation across 10+ Indian languages, a full Text Tools lab, face-to-face Live Translate, an AI Chat assistant, offline autocomplete, and a fully customizable keyboard experience.

Whether you're chatting on WhatsApp, writing emails, or posting on Instagram, TypeAura makes your words sound exactly the way you mean them — instantly, in any app.

AI Tools

Six powerful tools, one app

Tap the AI Tools tab inside TypeAura to access all six — Live Translate, Text Tools, AI Chat, Smart Reply, Email Composer, and Tone Changer.

Live Translate

Face-to-face real-time conversation

Place your phone between two people — speak in your language, the other person sees the translation on their side of the screen, spoken aloud automatically. Supports 10+ languages.

  • ✅ Auto-speak translated text

Text Tools

Write, fix, translate & transform

Paste any text and run it through 16+ AI tools — organized into three tabs so you always find what you need instantly.

Builder Story

Why I built TypeAura

I'm Himanshu Kashyap, a developer who's been frustrated with keyboards that don't understand how Indians actually type. We all mix Hindi and English constantly, but every keyboard out there treats it like a problem instead of a superpower.

So I built TypeAura — a keyboard that doesn't judge your Hinglish, but makes it shine. With AI translation built right into the keyboard, you can type the way you think and sound the way you want.

TypeAura is in beta and improving every week. Your usage and feedback directly shapes what gets built next.
